## About Me

I've been working in the cybersecurity industry for over a decade, with a consistent emphasis on detecting and responding to attacks. In that time, I have remediated large incidents in a global telecommunications company, made key forensics contributions to complex breaches, hunted down hackers in large enterprise networks, and contributed novel research across network, endpoint, and cloud security domains.

Across all my experience, I have found a particular love of overlooked detection areas. Over the last several years I have spent time across Linux endpoints, container/Kubernetes, and the cloud control plane.

In addition to that, I have a strong track record of development and automation skills; I have built quick scripts to solve day-to-day problems inside a SOC, automated workflows using deterministic and ML/LLM-based tools, and worked with an engineering org to design a cloud control plane detection engine from the ground up for an international MDR provider.

I'm a highly experienced detection engineer, and I love working on a team with a broader goal to ensure safety in computer networks.
